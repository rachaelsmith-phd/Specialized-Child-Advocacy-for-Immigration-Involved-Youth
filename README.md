# Specialized Child Advocacy for Immigration-Involved Youth

A research, policy, and curriculum-development initiative exploring best-interests advocacy models for immigration-involved children.

This project examines how child-centered advocacy frameworks may support children involved in immigration proceedings, particularly where child welfare, legal systems, education, healthcare, and community services intersect.

---

## Areas of Focus

- Child Advocacy
- Immigration Law
- Child Welfare
- Best Interests Determinations
- Special Immigrant Juvenile Status (SIJS)
- Unaccompanied and Accompanied Minors
- Trauma-Informed Practice
- Interagency Collaboration
- Curriculum Development
- Policy Innovation
