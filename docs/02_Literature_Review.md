# Chapter 2: Literature Review

## I. Introduction

### A. Background and Context

- Emergence of child-centered advocacy in American legal systems
- Growth of immigration-related child populations
- Increasing intersection of immigration law and child welfare systems
- Need for specialized advocacy for vulnerable children

### B. Purpose of the Literature Review

This literature review seeks to:

- Examine the historical development of child advocacy
- Explore federal immigration laws affecting children
- Analyze existing advocacy and representation models
- Identify gaps in best-interests representation
- Establish a foundation for a proposed Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Framework

### C. Guiding Research Questions

1. How has child advocacy evolved within the United States legal system?
2. How are children's interests currently represented in immigration proceedings?
3. What protections currently exist for immigrant children?
4. What gaps remain in child-centered advocacy?
5. Can a CASA-informed advocacy framework address those gaps?
