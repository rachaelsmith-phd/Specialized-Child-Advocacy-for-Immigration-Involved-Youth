# Publication & Manuscript Development Portfolio
## Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Initiative

This publication agenda outlines a long-term scholarly program of research focused on child advocacy, immigration systems, child welfare, curriculum development, and public policy.

The portfolio moves from conceptual framework development to pilot implementation, evaluation, and eventual book-length scholarship.

---

# Manuscript 1
## Conceptual Article

### Best Interests Child Advocacy in Immigration Proceedings:
### Toward a CASA-Informed Framework

**Manuscript Type**

Conceptual Framework Article

### Purpose

Introduce the Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) concept as a child-centered advocacy framework for immigration-involved youth.

### Core Topics

- CASA and GAL models
- Child welfare principles
- Best interests doctrine
- Immigration systems
- Interdisciplinary advocacy
- Proposed BICA framework

### Target Journals

- *Children and Youth Services Review*

- *Family Court Review*

The BICA publication portfolio advances a multidisciplinary research agenda situated at the intersection of:

- Child Welfare
- Immigration Systems
- Criminal Justice
- Curriculum & Instruction
- Adult Learning
- Public Policy
- Interagency Collaboration

Collectively, these manuscripts provide a pathway from conceptual scholarship to practical implementation, supporting the development of an evidence-informed framework for advocating on behalf of immigration-involved children.
