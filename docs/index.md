## Emerging Research & Policy Initiatives

### Best Interests Advocacy for Immigration-Involved Children
**A CASA-Informed Framework for Federal, State, and Community Collaboration**

📋 **Project Status:** Concept Development

This initiative explores the development of a **Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Framework**, a child-centered advocacy model informed by Court Appointed Special Advocate (CASA) principles and adapted for immigration-involved children.

The project examines opportunities to strengthen collaboration among immigration systems, child welfare agencies, courts, schools, healthcare providers, and community organizations while ensuring that children's developmental, educational, medical, and family-related needs remain visible throughout legal proceedings.

#### Planned Components

- Process mapping of immigration-involved child pathways
- Identification of advocacy gaps and intervention points
- Development of a specialized advocate training curriculum
- Best-interest assessment framework
- Interagency collaboration model
- Pilot program and evaluation framework
- Policy and implementation recommendations

#### Research Areas

- Child welfare
- Immigration policy
- CASA/GAL advocacy models
- Trauma-informed practice
- Family reunification
- Educational continuity
- Interagency collaboration
- Best-interest determinations

> **Current Phase:** Conceptual development and framework design. Future work may include process mapping, curriculum development, pilot testing, and grant-supported research initiatives.

---

*This project is part of a broader research agenda focused on child welfare, justice systems, public policy, and community-centered advocacy.*
