# Long-Term Vision

The presentation portfolio serves as a bridge between research, policy, and practice by advancing conversation around child-centered advocacy for immigration-involved youth. Through academic conferences, professional trainings, policy briefings, and interdisciplinary collaboration, these presentations support the development of a scalable and evidence-informed Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Framework.
