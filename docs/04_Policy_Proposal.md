# Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Initiative

## Executive Summary

The **Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA) Initiative** proposes the development and pilot implementation of a specialized child advocacy framework for immigration-involved youth. Drawing upon the successful principles of Court Appointed Special Advocates (CASA) and Guardian ad Litem (GAL) programs, the initiative seeks to strengthen child-centered decision-making within federal immigration systems.

The proposed framework would provide trained advocates whose primary responsibility is to assess, document, and communicate the best interests of children involved in immigration proceedings. The model aims to improve interagency collaboration, enhance service coordination, and provide courts and agencies with independent assessments focused on child well-being.

---

# Problem Statement

Federal immigration systems and child welfare systems operate under distinct legal mandates.

### Immigration Systems Prioritize

- Enforcement
- Legal status determination
- Admissibility
- Relief eligibility

### Child Welfare Systems Prioritize

- Safety
- Permanency
- Well-being

Children involved in immigration proceedings often interact with both systems simultaneously. Despite this overlap, no universally available advocate exists whose sole responsibility is to assess and communicate the child's best interests across all stages of the immigration process.

As a result, educational, developmental, medical, mental health, and family-related considerations may not be consistently represented within federal decision-making processes.

---

# Policy Goal

Establish a pilot **Best Interests Child Advocate (BICA)** model that:

- Complements legal representation
- Strengthens child-centered decision-making
- Improves interagency communication
- Provides structured advocacy training
- Supports evidence-informed policy development

---

# Program Objectives

## Objective 1
### Develop a National BICA Training Framework

Create a competency-based training curriculum that prepares advocates to work effectively within immigration, child welfare, educational, healthcare, and court systems.

---

## Objective 2
### Pilot Advocacy Services for Immigration-Involved Youth

Implement and evaluate child-centered advocacy services within selected jurisdictions serving accompanied and unaccompanied children.

---

## Objective 3
### Improve Interagency Communication

Strengthen collaboration among:

- Department of Homeland Security (DHS)
- Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR)
- State Child Welfare Agencies
- Immigration Courts
- Schools
- Healthcare Providers
- Community Organizations

---

## Objective 4
### Evaluate Outcomes and Program Effectiveness

Measure the impact of advocacy services on child well-being, court processes, and interagency coordination.

# Long-Term Vision

The long-term goal of the BICA Initiative is to establish a sustainable, evidence-informed framework for independent child advocacy within immigration systems.

By adapting proven child welfare advocacy principles to the unique needs of immigration-involved youth, the initiative seeks to strengthen child-centered decision-making while enhancing collaboration among federal agencies, courts, schools, healthcare providers, community organizations, and families.
